Tinted-Theming-LICENSE.txt. Palette author credits and the original YAML files
are retained in the matching Clumsier source archive.

The same release/download location must also offer the dependency-sources ZIP.
It contains matching Qt Base, Qt Declarative, Qt Tools, and WinDivert source
archives, plus their origin URLs and SHA-256 checksums. Clumsier's own source
ZIP includes its build scripts, cJSON source, and palette sources. Building
Clumsier does not require rebuilding Qt or the signed WinDivert driver.
